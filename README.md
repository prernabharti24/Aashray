# 🏠 AASHRAY – Mitti Se Mausam Tak

### AI-Powered Area-Specific Thermal Comfort & Smart Shelter Design Platform

AASHRAY is a smart shelter design platform that helps users design **location-specific, climate-responsive shelters** by analyzing environmental and weather conditions such as temperature, humidity, wind, solar radiation, and rainfall.

The goal is simple: **instead of designing the same shelter everywhere, AASHRAY recommends shelter solutions according to the climate and conditions of a particular area.**

---

## 🌱 Problem Statement

Traditional shelters such as bus stops, waiting areas, worker shelters, and public spaces are often designed using generic layouts without sufficiently considering the local climate.

A shelter that provides good thermal comfort in one location may become uncomfortable in another because of differences in:

- 🌡️ Temperature
- 💧 Humidity
- 🌬️ Wind direction and speed
- ☀️ Solar radiation
- 🌧️ Rainfall
- 🌤️ Seasonal conditions
- 📍 Geographic location

This creates a need for a software-based system that can recommend **area-specific shelter designs for better thermal comfort**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

AASHRAY analyzes the environmental conditions of a selected location and generates recommendations for designing a suitable shelter.

The platform can help determine:

- Suitable shelter orientation
- Roof and shading requirements
- Open/closed side configuration
- Ventilation requirements
- Material recommendations
- Thermal comfort considerations
- Rain and weather protection
- Suitable shelter type for the location

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📍 Location-Based Analysis
Uses the selected/current location to obtain environmental and weather information.

### 🌦️ Live Weather Data
Fetches relevant weather parameters such as:

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Wind speed
- Wind direction
- Rainfall
- Solar/environmental conditions

### 🧠 Smart Shelter Recommendations
Analyzes environmental conditions and recommends suitable shelter characteristics.

### 🌡️ Thermal Comfort Analysis
Evaluates how environmental conditions can affect the thermal comfort of people using the shelter.

### 🏗️ Area-Specific Design
Generates recommendations according to the characteristics of the selected location rather than using a single generic shelter model.

### 🏠 Temporary & Permanent Shelter Support

**Temporary Structures**
- Bus shelters
- Worker shelters
- Event shelters
- Waiting shelters

**Permanent Structures**
- Public waiting areas
- Community spaces
- Outdoor architectural structures

### 📊 Design & Analysis Reports
Presents environmental analysis and recommended design parameters in an understandable format.

### 🧊 3D Visualization
Provides a 3D representation of the proposed shelter architecture to help users visualize the recommended design.
